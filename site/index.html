A little more recall

Small details.
Big relief.

Your clipboard history should feel instant, stay out of the way, and keep your information yours.

Search your whole history

Type a word or fragment to bring a clip back into view. Results are ordered by recency, and the list keeps loading as you explore.

Bring a clip back

Use the arrows to choose, then press Return or click. The clip goes onto your clipboard, ready to paste.

Images included

Keep image clips alongside text, see thumbnails in history, and open a larger preview when you need it.

QR codes, both ways

Turn a text clip into a scannable QR image, or scan a QR code in an image clip and copy its contents.

Keep your favorites

Star the clips you want to keep close. Favorites are protected when older history is trimmed.

Find it by time

Filter history by a recent time window, from the past hour to the last week.

Pause whenever

Temporarily stop capture from the menu bar, with a clear status indicator so you know when recording is paused.

Your history belongs to you

Engram stores history locally. Set a retention limit, exclude recognized sensitive clipboard types, and decide when recording is on.

Your clipboard is personal

Private, on purpose.

Your history lives in a local database on your Mac. No account, no sync service, no telemetry. Engram also skips recognized concealed and transient pasteboard content.

Back to your flow

Copy. Find. Carry on.

No new habit to learn — just a little more memory when you need it.

Copy like you always do

Text and images are captured in the background. Pause recording from the menu bar any time.

Open your history

Press ⌥⌘V or click the menu-bar icon. Search, filter, or browse older clips.

Choose and paste

Press Return to put the selected clip on your clipboard, then paste it into the app you were using.
