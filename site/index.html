Features

Built to feel instant

One SQLite database is the only source of truth. Search stays fast as your history grows — no in-memory mirror, no cloud.

⚡

Instant search

Search your entire history as you type. Word-prefix search is served by a full-text index that stays sub-millisecond at 100,000 clips.

invoice
⌘

Keyboard-first

Arrows to move, Enter to paste, Esc to dismiss. Your hands never leave the keyboard.

🧠

Memory with context

Every clip remembers where it came from and when, with relative timestamps.

🔒

Private by design

Skips password managers. Stored locally in SQLite — no cloud, no account.

🍃

Lightweight

A native menu-bar app. No Electron, no telemetry, near-zero CPU when idle.

∞

No limits

Keep unlimited history, or cap it. Duplicate copies bump to the top instead of piling up.

12 MB
🔒

Everything stays on your Mac.

Engram never phones home. Your history lives in a local SQLite database, password-manager clips are skipped, and nothing you copy is ever sent to a server.

Local only No account No telemetry Skips passwords

Install in seconds.

Open the disk image, drag Engram into Applications, and keep copying as usual — Engram remembers it all.
