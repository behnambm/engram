Instant search

Search your entire history as you type. It stays fast at 100,000 clips, not just 100.

Keyboard-first

Arrows to move, Enter to paste, Esc to dismiss. Your hands never leave the keyboard.

Memory with context

Every clip remembers where it came from and when, with relative timestamps.

Private by design

Skips password managers and stores everything locally in SQLite. No cloud, no account.

∞

No limits

Keep unlimited history, or cap it — duplicate copies bump to the top instead of piling up.

🍃

Lightweight

A native menu-bar app. No Electron, no telemetry, near-zero CPU when idle.

Install in seconds

Open the disk image and drag Engram into Applications. Then copy as usual — Engram remembers it all.
